Tomorrow, worn today.

Domani means tomorrow. We make parfums for the person you are becoming. Concentrated, considered, and made to be worn, not saved for a special day.

In Italian, domani means tomorrow. We chose it because a fragrance is never only about the moment you spray it. It is about who walks into the room next: the meeting, the evening, the version of you that has not happened yet. Every DOMANI parfum is made with that tomorrow in mind.
