grace. vs. Graswald: Two Ways to Scale Fashion Imagery with AI

Graswald builds digital twins for enterprise product imagery. grace. is the AI fashion studio for brand-defining campaigns, lookbooks, and video. Where each approach shines.

Graswald AI is one of the more serious players in AI product imagery: an enterprise platform that creates digital twins of garments and generates photorealistic PDP shots, flatlays, and campaign content for global fashion brands. If you are evaluating Graswald, you should also look at grace. — the two take related but distinct approaches. Here is how they compare.

What Graswald Does Well

Graswald builds digital twins: photorealistic 3D models of your products, generated for example from a short video capture, which then produce imagery at enterprise scale — on-model PDP shots in 4K, custom AI models, custom lighting and poses, and native integrations into PIM and DAM systems. Its positioning is enterprise workflow automation: getting entire collections online in days instead of months.

Where grace. Takes a Different Path

Creative Direction First

grace. is built as an AI fashion studio, not primarily as a pipeline tool. The core promise is brand-defining visual content: campaign imagery, lookbooks, and e-commerce shots that carry your Brand DNA — your fits, your color palettes, your model looks, your aesthetic — consistently across everything you publish.

Moving and Still Visuals

grace. produces video and stills with the same identity and creative direction. For brands whose channels live on motion — social, retail screens, digital campaigns — this is a structural advantage over photo-first pipelines.

From 3D Sample Directly Into the Collection

Like Graswald, grace. connects to the 3D world — but with a focus on speed to market: grace. works directly from 3D samples, so collection visuals can exist before the first physical sample is sewn. Production time drops from weeks to hours, and brands report an average cost reduction of 75 percent versus traditional photoshoot production.

Accessible Beyond Enterprise

Graswald is positioned for enterprise workflows. grace. offers a no-code studio that creative teams operate themselves — from emerging labels to established brands — with a personal demo and a quote tailored to content volume rather than enterprise procurement.

Why Choose grace.

grace. covers the full spectrum — from high-volume e-commerce production to brand-defining campaign work:

  • Scale without losing identity: entire collections rendered with one consistent Brand DNA, from PDP shots to campaign imagery.
  • Stills and video from one studio: moving and still visuals with the same identity, speed, and creative direction.
  • Fastest path to market: directly from 3D samples into sellable content — production in hours instead of weeks, at an average cost reduction of 75 percent.
  • Operated by your own team: a no-code studio for designers and creative directors, from emerging labels to established brands — no enterprise procurement required to get started.
