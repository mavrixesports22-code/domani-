Keeping your parfum at its best

Where to keep your bottle, how to care for the cap and why a drawer is better than a bathroom shelf.

A cool, dry place

Keep your bottle in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight, heat and flame. Close the cap after use. A drawer or cupboard is better than a bathroom shelf.

Care for the discovery vials

Keep the vials upright, away from heat and sunlight. The Panther Discovery Set is designed to let you wear each scent on your own skin before choosing a full-size bottle.

Read the package

Ingredient lists and safety information may change from time to time. Please refer to the information on the product package you receive.

Keep fragrance for its intended use

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes and irritated skin. Keep away from heat and flame. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children.
