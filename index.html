Campaigns, lookbooks and product shots at scale

An AI fashion studio for brands: campaign imagery, lookbooks and product shots from your own garments. No studio day, no casting, no location.

What is grace ai?+

grace ai is an AI fashion studio: software that turns your own garments into photorealistic on-model imagery, lookbooks, campaigns and video. Your team picks the model, the scene and the light, and has the first visual in minutes.

How much does grace ai cost?+

About 80 percent below a studio shoot with models, crew and logistics, and a finished visual in about 3 minutes. Pricing follows your volume; the team quotes it in a demo.

Can I use my own products and models with grace ai?+

Yes. Upload a packshot, a flat lay or a ghost-mannequin shot and it comes back on a model in minutes. Create exclusive AI models that belong only to your brand, so the face in the lookbook is the face in the ads.

Does the same model stay consistent across a whole campaign?+

Yes. Once you pick a model, that model stays identical across every look, channel and scene. That is where most AI imagery fails; here it is the point.

How realistic are the visuals grace ai creates?+

Built for the pages where a bad seam gets noticed: product pages, lookbooks and campaigns. Fabric, drape and print hold, and you set background, lighting and styling shot by shot.

Does grace ai create video as well as photos?+

Yes. Any key visual becomes motion for Reels, TikTok and stories, with the same model and garment as the still. No separate video day.

Can grace ai generate flatlays?+

Yes. The same product input that creates on-model imagery also creates flatlays and still-life shots, so product page, lookbook and campaign come from one run.
