What is DOMANI?+

DOMANI is a unisex luxury perfume house. In Italian, domani means tomorrow. Five distinctive parfums are built for skin, not shelves.

How much do DOMANI parfums cost?+

Each 50 ml parfum is ₹1,199. The Panther Discovery Set is ₹299 and contains five 2 ml vials. Current prices and offers are shown in the collection.

Can I try all five fragrances first?+

Yes. The Panther Discovery Set brings together all five DOMANI parfums in 2 ml vials. Wear each scent on your own skin through a full day and discover which one feels like you.

How long does the fragrance last?+

DOMANI parfums contain 25% perfume oil. Typical wear is 8 to 10 hours; longevity depends on your skin, climate and application.

Are DOMANI fragrances unisex?+

Yes. All five DOMANI parfums are unisex. Choose by the notes and the mood you enjoy, from fresh and aquatic to warm, woody or gourmand.

What are the delivery options?+

Free shipping is available on orders above ₹999. Orders are processed in 24–36 working hours after confirmation, with delivery in 5–7 working days after dispatch. COD is offered for eligible pincodes at checkout.

How can I contact DOMANI?+

For product or order support, email support@domani.in. Find DOMANI on Instagram at @domani.perfume or use the contact links on the official store.
