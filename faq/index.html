What is DOMANI?

DOMANI is a unisex luxury perfume house. In Italian, domani means tomorrow. We make parfums for the person you are becoming: concentrated, considered, and made to be worn, not saved for a special day.

Are DOMANI fragrances unisex?

Yes. Scent has no gender. Every DOMANI fragrance is made to be worn by anyone who chooses it. Choose the notes and mood you enjoy.

How long do DOMANI parfums last?

Typical wear is 8 to 10 hours. Longevity varies with your skin, climate and application. Wear each fragrance through a full day to discover how it develops on you.

What is the perfume oil concentration?

DOMANI parfums are made with 25% perfume oil. The house standard is concentration over decoration, so the fragrance unfolds slowly on skin.

How do I apply my parfum?

On clean skin, spray once or twice on pulse points such as the neck and wrists, 15–20 cm away from the body. Do not rub.

How should I store my parfum?

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, heat and flame. Keep out of reach of children. Refer to your product package for current ingredient and safety information.
