Frequently asked questions

What grace ai does, who it is for, and how it works.

What is grace ai?

grace ai is software for fashion brands. Your team uploads garments or 3D samples, picks a model and a scene, and creates photorealistic stills and video for campaigns, lookbooks, e-commerce and social. No shoot, no model booking, no studio day. The models are trained on fashion, so fabric, fit and drape hold up under scrutiny.

Is grace ai a photo studio or a software product?

Software. Nobody at grace ai takes a brief or produces visuals for you. Your team logs in and creates the visuals itself, usually with a first look within minutes. The name describes the studio you run, not a service you order.

How much does a fashion photoshoot cost compared to AI?

A campaign shoot typically runs between EUR 50,000 and EUR 500,000 once models, location, crew and reshoots are counted, and it takes weeks to plan. Brands using grace ai report about 80% lower cost per campaign, and hours instead of weeks.

How much does grace ai cost?

Pricing depends on how many visuals you produce and whether you need exclusive models. The team quotes it in a personal demo, which you can book at https://gracefashion.ai/book. Measured against a shoot, brands report about 80% lower cost per campaign.

Can AI turn flat product shots into on-model images?

Yes. Upload the packshot or ghost-mannequin shot, choose a model and a scene, and the garment is on a body in minutes. The same garment then runs through as many variations as you need: new pose, new location, new model.

Does the AI model stay the same across every image?

Yes. Once you choose a model, that model stays identical in the studio, on location and in motion. This is where most AI imagery fails and why brands stop after one test. With grace ai the lookbook, the ads and the Reels show the same face.

Can AI models show different body shapes and skin tones?

Yes. You choose from every body shape, age and skin tone, or define exclusive models that only your brand uses. Casting becomes a setting, not a booking.

Can AI create fashion video for Reels and TikTok?

Yes. Any still becomes a short clip: the model walks, turns, the fabric moves. Model and garment stay consistent with your stills, so there is no separate video production day.

Can we create visuals before the physical sample exists?

Yes. grace ai works from 3D samples, so campaign, lookbook and shop imagery can be ready before the first piece is sewn. Sample production and photography drop off the critical path to market.

How fast can we produce a lookbook with AI?

The first visual takes minutes. A complete lookbook takes about 24 hours, compared to several weeks for a shoot with planning, casting and retouching.

Do we have to label AI-generated fashion images?

In the EU, the transparency rules of the AI Act have applied since August 2026. They ask for AI-generated images to be recognisable as such where they could be taken for real photographs. Many brands add a short note in the caption or on the product page. How you label is the brand’s decision.

Do we need technical or AI expertise to use grace ai?

No. Designers and marketers work directly in the tool. There is no prompt engineering to learn and no engineering support needed.

How does grace ai compare to Botika and other AI fashion tools?

Most tools are built for one shot: the garment on a model against white. grace ai is built for the whole campaign: the same model across stills, video, studio and location, plus exclusive models and 3D-sample input. Side-by-side comparisons with Botika, Graswald and Nano Banana are at https://gracefashion.ai/blog.
